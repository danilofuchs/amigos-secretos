# Amigos Secretos: paper ritual

Mode: Operate. Routes: creation and management, with the same material world across home, list and private tickets.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Recreate the user's actual amigo secreto ritual: notebook paper, blue ballpoint names, hand-torn horizontal strips and two vertical folds. Organization is a readable notebook, receiving is an unfolding paper interaction.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white ruled sheets, a light desaturated kraft-paper backing with photographic fiber texture, blue #2146a0 handwriting with pigment skips and pressure variation, red notebook margins and natural ambient paper shadows. Public Sans labels and self-hosted Caveat writing. No gift mascots, serif marketing headings, eyebrow labels or dashboard-card grid.

STORY: The organizer writes the meeting and rules on an open notebook, pastes participants and handles their torn paper slips. Optional exclusions open inline. Each recipient opens a horizontal strip in two vertical folds (quarter to half to full), revealing lettering printed on the moving paper itself, with faint mirrored ink showing through its reverse. WhatsApp sharing remains explicit and per person.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At desktop, a small blue handwritten wordmark and restrained navigation sit above a large notebook sheet on the left and a narrow stack of participant slips on the right. Title and rules occupy actual ruled writing areas; people have a visible name entry and count. At mobile the notebook and participants stack in task order. The primary create action follows the participants and optional exclusions, with a count explaining disabled state.
